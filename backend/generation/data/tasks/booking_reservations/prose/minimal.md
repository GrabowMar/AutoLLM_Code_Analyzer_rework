Build a restaurant reservation app. Each time slot seats at most 20 guests. Anyone can see a day's booked slots and remaining seats; signed-in users book, change and cancel their own reservations, and the admin sees all reservations.

Endpoints: POST /api/auth/register, POST /api/auth/login, POST /api/reservations, GET /api/reservations, GET /api/reservations/{id}, PUT /api/reservations/{id}, DELETE /api/reservations/{id}, GET /api/availability, GET /api/admin/reservations.
