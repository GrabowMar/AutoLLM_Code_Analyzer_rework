Build a public notes board. Signed-in users write notes with a title, plain-text content and up to ten tags, pin important ones, and edit or delete their own; anyone browses notes by tag, author or text and sees the most used tags.

Endpoints: POST /api/auth/register, POST /api/auth/login, GET /api/notes, POST /api/notes, GET /api/notes/{id}, PATCH /api/notes/{id}, DELETE /api/notes/{id}, GET /api/tags.
