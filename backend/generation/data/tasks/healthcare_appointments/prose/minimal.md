Build a clinic appointment app. Signed-in users book, reschedule, complete and cancel their own appointments; a public schedule shows how many appointments each time of a day has, without patient details; the admin sees all appointments.

Endpoints: POST /api/auth/register, POST /api/auth/login, GET /api/appointments, POST /api/appointments, GET /api/appointments/{id}, PATCH /api/appointments/{id}, DELETE /api/appointments/{id}, GET /api/schedule, GET /api/admin/appointments.
