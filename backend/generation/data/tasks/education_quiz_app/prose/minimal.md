Build a multiple-choice quiz app. Anyone can draw a random quiz and submit answers for a score with a review of every question; signed-in users keep a history of their attempts. The admin adds questions.

Endpoints: POST /api/auth/register, POST /api/auth/login, GET /api/quiz, POST /api/attempts, GET /api/attempts, GET /api/attempts/{id}, POST /api/admin/questions, GET /api/admin/questions.
