Build a marketing campaign monitor. Anyone can browse campaigns and their daily performance; signed-in users create campaigns, record daily figures (impressions, clicks, conversions, spend, revenue) and change a campaign's status. The app shows spend, conversions and budget pacing.

Endpoints: POST /api/auth/register, POST /api/auth/login, GET /api/campaigns, POST /api/campaigns, GET /api/campaigns/{id}, PATCH /api/campaigns/{id}, GET /api/campaigns/{id}/performance, POST /api/campaigns/{id}/performance.
